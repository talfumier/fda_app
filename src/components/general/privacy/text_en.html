Privacy

Collected data

When visiting www.festivaldesarts.merville31.fr, no personal data are collected from regular visitors.

Artists who wish to exhibit must create a personal account. In this context, personal data are collected: name, surname, email address, postal address, and phone number.

The processing of these data is intended to manage registrations and/or promote artists on the website, solely with their consent. Data are retained only as long as necessary to provide the requested service.

Data are never transferred or sold to third parties.

In accordance with the GDPR, you have the following rights:

  • The right to withdraw your consent at any time;
  • The right to access, correct, or delete your data;
  • The right to restrict or object to data processing.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact the Data Protection Officer by email at:

rgpd@merville31.fr

Cookies and connection data

The website uses strictly necessary technical cookies to access the member area, while the public website requires none.

In accordance with Article 82 of the French “Informatique et Libertés” law and the GDPR, these cookies do not require user consent.

These cookies are not used for advertising or analytics purposes but solely to ensure secure and personalized navigation.

In particular, the site stores, within a secure token (JSON Web Token):

  • The login identifier (user’s email address);
  • The preferred browsing language.

This information is stored in a cookie for the purposes of authentication and language personalization.

The cookie is valid for 3 hours, after which it automatically expires.

These data are neither sold nor shared nor used for any other purpose. No advertising or audience measurement cookies are placed.


You may delete cookies at any time via your browser settings.
